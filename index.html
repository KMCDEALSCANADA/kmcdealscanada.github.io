GOOD TO KNOW

How shopping works.

Where do I buy the products?

Browse products at KMC Deals Canada, then visit the retailer’s website to purchase. We do not operate a checkout or sell merchandise directly.

Who charges me and delivers my order?

The retailer you choose processes payment and ships your order. KMC Deals Canada never receives, stocks or ships the products.

How do I request a return, exchange or refund?

Contact the retailer you purchased from directly, using your order confirmation and their return policy. For example, if you purchased from Garmin, contact Garmin or the actual seller shown on your receipt—not KMC Deals Canada.

Who handles warranty claims and order questions?

The retailer or manufacturer, as specified in the purchase terms, handles warranty requests and after-sales support. Keep your proof of purchase and check the policy before ordering.
